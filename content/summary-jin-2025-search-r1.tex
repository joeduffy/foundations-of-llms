% Paper Summary: Search-R1: Training LLMs to Reason and Leverage Search Engines with Reinforcement Learning (Jin et al., 2025)

\begin{papersummary}{2025}{Search-R1: Training LLMs to Reason and Leverage Search Engines with Reinforcement Learning}{Bowen Jin, Hansi Zeng, Zhenrui Yue, Jinsung Yoon, Sercan \"O. Ar\i k, Dong Wang, Hamed Zamani, Jiawei Han}{Search-R1 extended reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards from closed reasoning to multi-turn tool use, training models to interleave search queries with reasoning under an outcome-only reward and establishing the recipe for agentic reinforcement learning.}

\summaryheading{Key Ideas}
Reasoning models trained in the style of \hyperref[paper:deepseek-2025-r1]{DeepSeek-R1 (2025)} think in a closed loop; agents must act in an open one, where each tool call injects text the model did not write. Search-R1 trains a model that issues search queries mid-reasoning, reads the retrieved passages, and continues---over as many rounds as the problem requires---using only a final-answer reward. Its central technical device is retrieved-token masking: tokens returned by the search engine are excluded from the policy-gradient loss, so the model is optimized only over what it chose to generate, which proved essential for stable training. The recipe works under both PPO (\hyperref[paper:schulman-2017-ppo]{Schulman et al., 2017}) and GRPO (\hyperref[paper:shao-2024-grpo]{Shao et al., 2024}), and improved multi-hop question answering by 41\% over retrieval-augmented baselines at 7B scale, with the model learning when to search, what to ask, and how to revise queries---behaviors specified nowhere in the reward.

\summaryheading{Follow-on Works}
The mask-tool-outputs, reward-outcomes recipe became the substrate of agentic reinforcement learning. Deep-research systems trained models to browse, read, and synthesize over long horizons; software agents were trained end-to-end against test-suite rewards in executable environments; and frontier models from Kimi K2 through K3 scaled the paradigm to sandboxed multi-turn training with persistent environment state at million-token context. The approach extended retrieval-augmented generation (\hyperref[paper:lewis-2020]{Lewis et al., 2020}) from a fixed inference-time architecture into a learned behavior, and refinements of turn-level credit assignment became an active research line.

\summaryheading{Lasting Contributions}
Search-R1 marks the point where the verifiable-rewards paradigm (\hyperref[paper:lambert-2024-tulu3]{Lambert et al., 2024}) crossed from reasoning into agency: the same outcome-driven optimization that taught models to think longer taught them to act, observe, and act again. Retrieved-token masking resolved the question of how policy gradients should treat text the environment wrote, a convention now assumed throughout agentic training, and the demonstration that tool-use strategy emerges from outcome rewards alone did for agents what R1-Zero had done for chains of thought.

\end{papersummary}
