\PlainChapter{Revision History}

\noindent\textbf{v1.0.0 --- February 18, 2026.}\quad Initial release: 71 papers in seven
chronological parts and three appendices, spanning 1943--2025.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{v2.0.0 --- August 1, 2026.}\quad Added Part VIII, \emph{Reasoning and the Open
Frontier} (2024--2026), collecting the foundational methods of the reasoning era: GRPO,
multi-token prediction, auxiliary-loss-free load balancing, T\"ulu~3 and verifiable rewards, Muon,
Kimi Delta Attention, and LatentMoE, with DeepSeek-R1 moved into the new part from the former
system-reports appendix. Added knowledge distillation, AdamW,
RMSNorm, speculative decoding, and LLM-QAT to Parts III--VII, and extended the agents appendix
through the agentic reinforcement-learning era with Reflexion, SWE-agent, and Search-R1. Adopted a stricter selection policy
under which integrated model reports and system cards are cited rather than reproduced, removing
the former system-reports appendix (GPT-4, Gemini~1.5, DeepSeek-V3, o1, GPT-5) together with
Llama~2 and Mixtral, whose lasting techniques are documented by embedded method papers. Corrected
the LLaMA facsimile, which had duplicated Llama~2. Revised the prologue and epilogue for the
reasoning era and corrected several era ranges.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{v2.0.1 --- August 1, 2026.}\quad Fixed a structural bug, present since the
initial release, in which every table-of-contents entry and cross-reference for an individual
paper linked to the entry immediately preceding it rather than to itself, because the anchor was
created before the page break that began the paper's own page rather than after it.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{v2.1.0 --- August 1, 2026.}\quad A typography and layout pass toward the
production standard of a bound mathematics monograph. Set the book twoside with mirrored inner and
outer margins and a canonical running head (page number on the outer edge; part title on the
verso, paper author and year on the recto); fixed a bug, also present since the initial release,
in which the running head froze after any unnumbered chapter and could show a stale or wrong
section name for hundreds of pages. Suppressed the book's own header and page number on facsimile
pages of the original papers, which had been competing with the source publication's own masthead
and pagination. Switched to single spacing. Replaced the saturated hyperlink blue throughout with
a muted, near-black ink color. Removed the version stamp and contact address from the title page
in favor of a proper copyright and colophon page. Shortened the part titles used in the table of
contents and running heads so they no longer wrap awkwardly. Added a back-of-book index of authors
and key technical terms.
