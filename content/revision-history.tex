\chapter*{Revision History}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Revision History}

\noindent\textbf{v1.0.0 --- February 18, 2026.}\quad Initial release: 71 papers in seven
chronological parts and three appendices, spanning 1943--2025.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{v2.0.0 --- August 1, 2026.}\quad Added Part VIII, \emph{Reasoning and the Open
Frontier} (2024--2026), collecting the foundational methods of the reasoning era: GRPO,
multi-token prediction, auxiliary-loss-free load balancing, T\"ulu~3 and verifiable rewards, Muon,
Kimi Delta Attention, and LatentMoE, with DeepSeek-R1 moved into the new part from the former
system-reports appendix. Added knowledge distillation, AdamW,
RMSNorm, speculative decoding, and LLM-QAT to Parts III--VII, and extended the agents appendix
through the agentic reinforcement-learning era with Reflexion, SWE-agent, and Search-R1. Adopted a stricter selection policy
under which integrated model reports and system cards are cited rather than reproduced, removing
the former system-reports appendix (GPT-4, Gemini~1.5, DeepSeek-V3, o1, GPT-5) together with
Llama~2 and Mixtral, whose lasting techniques are documented by embedded method papers. Corrected
the LLaMA facsimile, which had duplicated Llama~2. Revised the prologue and epilogue for the
reasoning era and corrected several era ranges.
