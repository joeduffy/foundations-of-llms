\PlainChapter{Revision History}

\noindent\textbf{First Edition --- February 18, 2026.}\quad 71 papers in seven chronological
parts and three appendices, spanning 1943--2025.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Second Edition --- August 1, 2026.}\quad Added Part VIII, \emph{Reasoning and
the Open Frontier} (2024--2026), collecting the foundational methods of the reasoning era: GRPO,
multi-token prediction, auxiliary-loss-free load balancing, T\"ulu~3 and verifiable rewards, Muon,
Kimi Delta Attention, and LatentMoE, with DeepSeek-R1 moved into the new part from the former
system-reports appendix. Added knowledge distillation, AdamW, RMSNorm, speculative decoding, and
LLM-QAT to Parts III--VII, and extended the agents appendix through the agentic
reinforcement-learning era with Reflexion, SWE-agent, and Search-R1. Adopted a stricter selection
policy under which integrated model reports and system cards are cited rather than reproduced,
removing the former system-reports appendix (GPT-4, Gemini~1.5, DeepSeek-V3, o1, GPT-5) together
with Llama~2 and Mixtral, whose lasting techniques are documented by embedded method papers.
Revised the prologue and epilogue for the reasoning era and corrected several era ranges.

Corrected two structural defects present since the first edition. Every table-of-contents entry
and cross-reference for an individual paper had linked to the entry immediately preceding it
rather than to itself, because the anchor was created before the page break that began the
paper's own page rather than after it. And the running head froze after any unnumbered chapter,
which could leave a stale or flatly wrong section name showing for hundreds of pages. Also
corrected the LLaMA facsimile, which had duplicated Llama~2.

Reset the book's typography and layout toward the production standard of a bound mathematics
monograph. Set twoside with mirrored inner and outer margins and a canonical running head (page
number on the outer edge; part title on the verso, paper author and year on the recto).
Suppressed the book's own header and page number on facsimile pages of the original papers, which
had been competing with the source publication's own masthead and pagination. Switched to single
spacing. Replaced the saturated hyperlink blue throughout with a muted, near-black ink color.
Removed the version stamp and contact address from the title page in favor of a proper copyright
and colophon page. Corrected the table of contents so a part's introduction nests visibly beneath
its part and above its papers, rather than sitting flush with one and unindented relative to the
other; shortened the part titles feeding the table of contents and running heads so they no
longer wrap awkwardly. Removed the forced blank page that had appeared between every part's title
page and its own introduction. Added a back-of-book index of authors and key technical terms.

Revised the prologue and epilogue as a matched pair rather than as two documents patched
independently on different schedules. The prologue's narrative previously ended at pretraining and
scale and never addressed alignment, reasoning, or agency, despite Part VIII and half of Appendix B
being devoted to exactly that; it now carries the same argument forward through preference
optimization, verifiable rewards, and trained agency. The epilogue previously omitted agents
entirely and closed on a general historical observation; it now opens by returning explicitly to
the prologue's central question, adds a section on the reliability of long-horizon agents, and
closes on a specific, still-open question about reasoning-chain faithfulness.
